What that means for a fork, or any changed version you share:

- **Give it another name and another icon.** Don't call it Tactile, or anything that could be taken for it, and don't use the Ripple or the menu-bar glyphs drawn from it.
- **Give it its own bundle identifier,** not `com.crissmoldovan.tactile`.
- **Give it its own update site and update keys,** or leave its updates off. It must not ask tactilemac.com for updates, or trust Tactile's update keys. [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) says what to change.

You may still say truthfully that your work is based on Tactile, and link here.

The official build is the one from [tactilemac.com](https://tactilemac.com), signed with Cristian Moldovan's Developer ID.
